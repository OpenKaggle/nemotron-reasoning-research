## Public Discussion Takeaways

- The six task families are stable between train and hidden test.
- You cannot change inference prompt or run a solver at evaluation time; all behavior must be learned into the LoRA.
- Strong public methods use deterministic chain-of-thought traces generated by symbolic solvers, then SFT/Tinker training.
- The biggest separation is bit manipulation. A public progress-prize writeup claims:
  - simple public validation baseline: 35/160 bit problems,
  - improved symbolic bit solver: 1364/1602 train bit problems,
  - target public score around 0.877.
- Numeral, gravity, and unit conversion are near-solvable with clean deterministic traces.
- Text cipher benefits from character-by-character mapping plus vocabulary fill.
- Symbolic equation/cipher-digit tasks need program search and carefully structured traces.

Kienngx's warm-start was untied, so mean-collapse destroyed it; that is exactly the cosine pattern observed (`up_proj.lora_A`: 0.15, `down_proj.lora_B`: 0.002).
